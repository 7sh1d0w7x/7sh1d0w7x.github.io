Questions

What server do I need?

Any Debian/Ubuntu VPS or bare-metal box (Hetzner, DigitalOcean, your host). I work over SSH — you don't have to do anything.

How do I pay?

Fixed price, agreed up front, milestone-based. You approve the scope before I start.

Will it break my running site?

Not silently. Every change is listed in writing before it happens, with its possible effect and the exact command that undoes it. Changes run inside a window you agree to, one type at a time, and I open a second session before touching anything that could affect access. Anything that can interrupt traffic is proposed separately — never folded into the same pass.

Can you do both — harden and automate?

Yes, that's the sweet spot: n8n self-hosted on a server that's actually locked down. Cheaper bundled.

Do you use my data?

I work over SSH on your server, and reports and evidence are sent to you directly by email. What I keep after delivery, and for how long, is agreed in writing before the engagement starts. This contact form has no backend — it only opens your email app.
