Questions

What server do I need?

Any Debian/Ubuntu VPS or bare-metal box (Hetzner, DigitalOcean, your host). I work over SSH — you don't have to do anything.

Fixed price, agreed up front, milestone-based. You approve the scope before I start.

No. I take a snapshot first, work read-only where possible, and document every change plus how to roll back.

Can you do both — harden and automate?

Yes, that's the sweet spot: n8n self-hosted on a server that's actually locked down. Cheaper bundled.

Do you use my data?

No. Everything stays on your infrastructure; nothing is routed through third parties.
